---
name: Links
summary: Each link says what kind it is (away, family, file, jump, email, phone, language, address, next, cross-reference), and an unsafe link is shown as text.
---
A link to [another site](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/ "MDN Web Docs") shows that site's icon, fetched once when the page was published, and a link to [Capsomer](https://capsomer.dustinedwards.info/) names the family site. A [relative link](/accessibility) is the site's own. A [picture file](/images/sample-640x360.svg) and a [data file](https://example.com/data.csv) say their type, and their size when the site knows it.

A [fragment](#el-headings) jumps on the page; a [mail link](mailto:someone@example.com) and a [phone link](tel:+15555550123) say what they open. :lang[[Leer en español](https://es.example.com/)]{hreflang=es} names its language in itself. A bare https://example.org/autolink, someone@example.com and the DOI [10.1000/182](https://doi.org/10.1000/182) are addresses. See :xref[[the headings](#el-headings)] marks a cross-reference.

[The accessibility statement](/accessibility)

An unsafe [script link](javascript:alert(1)) is not a link: it is shown as text.
